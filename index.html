About Wizlenny

Wizlenny, whose real name is Obinna Wisdom, is a Nigerian independent recording artist with a passion for creating meaningful music that inspires, uplifts, and connects with people. Guided by his motto, “Born for a Purpose 🦅,” he expresses his creativity, faith, experiences, and emotions through music.

Education and Background

Wizlenny began his early education at Living Word Academy. He later attended Bonus Pastor Seminary, Osina, and St. Mary's Seminary, Umuowa, for his secondary education and seminary formation.

He is currently studying Medical Laboratory Science at Rhema University while developing his independent music career.

Music and Purpose

Wizlenny explores different musical styles, including Christian music, Afrobeats, and love songs. His goal is to create music that resonates with listeners while remaining true to his values and artistic identity.
